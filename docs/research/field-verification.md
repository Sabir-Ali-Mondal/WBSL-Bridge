# WBSL Field Verification Report
## Interview Findings – West Bengal Sign Language (WBSL)

**Question prepared by:** Sabir Ali Mondal  
**Answers provided by:** Mahuya Sanyal Ma'am  
**Institution:** Maharani Nilima Probha Institute for the Deaf & Dumb, Berhampur  
**Answers documented by:** Sabir Ali Mondal  
**Original question language:** English  
**Interview language:** Bengali  

---

## 1. Language

**Q1. Which sign language is mainly used in West Bengal: ISL, regional/WBSL signs, BdSL, Home Sign, or mixed?**

**Answer:** Both WBSL and ISL are used, but WBSL is mainly used.

**Q2. Does the sign language vary by district/community?**

**Answer:** No.

**Q3. Which signs are different from standard ISL?**

**Answer:** Alphabets are different. ISL is otherwise the main reference.

## 2. Sign Structure

**Q4. Which body parts are most important for recognising signs: fingers, hands, palm/back of palm, wrist/arm, shoulder, head, face, mouth or body?**

**Answer:** Mouth, hands, fingers, face and head.

## 3. Timing and Movement

**Q5. Can the same sign be performed faster or slower with the same meaning?**

**Answer:** Yes.

**Q6. Can sign duration vary between people or situations?**

**Answer:** No.

**Q7. Can movement, direction, speed or repetition change meaning?**

**Answer:** No special variation was reported; movements were described as normal.

**Q8. How is the end of a sentence indicated?**

**Answer:** It can be indicated through a pause or stopping.

## 4. Grammar

**Q9. How are questions expressed?**

**Answer:** By using a question mark (?) sign.

**Q10. How is negation expressed: through signs, head movements, facial expressions or context?**

**Answer:** Through signs and normal head movements.

## 5. Face and Mouth

**Q11. How important are facial expressions in sign communication?**

**Answer:** Facial expressions are important.

**Q12. How important are head movements and head positions?**

**Answer:** Moderately important.

**Q13. Are mouth movements necessary for any signs?**

**Answer:** Mouth movements are used by normal hearing people during communication.

## 6. Emotion

**Q14. Does emotion change the meaning of a sign, or mainly express feelings and tone?**

**Answer:** No. Emotion does not change the sign's meaning.

**Q15. Which emotions are important to recognise?**

**Answer:** All major emotions, including sadness, happiness and anger.

**Q16. Can ignoring facial or emotional expressions cause misunderstanding?**

**Answer:** Yes, misunderstandings can happen.

## 7. Human Translation

**Q17. Does an interpreter translate sign-by-sign or according to meaning and context?**

**Answer:** Translation is performed slowly and continuously.

**Q18. How are differences between sign order and Bengali/English sentence order handled?**

**Answer:** The topic and surrounding context are signed first.

**Q19. How are unknown or regional signs handled?**

**Answer:** By showing an image or asking the person.

## 8. Bidirectional Communication

**Q20. At a doctor's chamber or hospital, how does Deaf ↔ hearing communication normally happen?**

**Answer:** Through normal communication methods.

**Q21. In court, police stations or legal situations, how is Deaf ↔ hearing communication handled?**

**Answer:** Only official and understandable information should be communicated. Guessing should be avoided.

**Q22. Is a human interpreter normally required?**

**Answer:** Yes.

**Q23. How does an interpreter handle Sign → Bengali/English translation?**

**Answer:** Through normal translation.

**Q24. How does an interpreter handle Bengali/English → Sign translation?**

**Answer:** Through normal translation.

**Q25. Is translation word-by-word or meaning-based?**

**Answer:** Both methods are used. Deaf people mainly understand and communicate through meaning.

**Q26. How is a hearing person's question converted into sign language?**

**Answer:** By using question signs and head movements.

**Q27. How is a Deaf person's answer converted into Bengali/English?**

**Answer:** Through normal translation.

## 9. Alphabet and Fingerspelling

**Q28. Which alphabet is used: Bengali, English or another?**

**Answer:** Specific alphabets are used. The exact alphabet system was not clarified.

**Q29. What is done for names, unknown words or words without existing signs?**

**Answer:** New signs may be created. Specific signs may be assigned to close people, and fingerspelling can also be used.

## 10. Regional Variation

**Q30. Can different people perform the same sign differently but mean the same thing?**

**Answer:** Yes.

**Q31. How should multiple signs with the same meaning be handled?**

**Answer:** They can be handled normally.

**Q32. How should an unknown or local sign be handled?**

**Answer:** Ask the person to understand the sign instead of guessing.

## 12. Research Implications for WBSL Bridge

Based on this interview, the following considerations may help guide the development of WBSL Bridge:

1. **Body Tracking:** The recognition system should consider hands, fingers, face, head and mouth rather than relying only on hand positions.

2. **Facial Expressions:** Facial information should be considered during sign recognition and communication.

3. **Unknown Sign Handling:** The system should avoid guessing when a sign cannot be recognised. Asking the user for clarification can help.

4. **Context-Based Translation:** Translation should consider context and meaning rather than depending entirely on word-by-word conversion.

5. **Regional Alphabet:** Alphabet differences should be investigated and documented separately.

6. **Sign Variations:** The dataset should consider different performances of the same sign.

7. **High-Risk Communication:** Medical and legal communication should not rely on uncertain automatic predictions.
