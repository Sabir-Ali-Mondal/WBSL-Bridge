WBSL Field Verification Questions

1. Language

1. Which sign language is mainly used in West Bengal: ISL, regional/WBSL signs, BdSL, Home Sign, or mixed?
2. Does the sign language vary by district/community?
3. Which signs are different from standard ISL?

2. Sign Structure

4. Which body parts are most important: fingers, hand, palm/back palm, wrist/arm, shoulder, head, face, mouth, body?
5. Which parts are essential and which are optional/additional?
6. Can signs be static, or do they require movement?
7. Which signs must be continuous/moving?

3. Timing & Movement

8. Can the same sign be performed faster/slower with the same meaning?
9. Can sign duration vary between people/situations?
10. Can movement, direction, speed or repetition change meaning?
11. Are there pauses/breaks between signs or sentences?
12. How is the end of a sentence indicated?

4. Grammar

13. How are questions expressed?
14. Are questions shown by sign, face, head movement, or combination?
15. How is negation expressed: sign, head movement, face, or context?
16. Can the same hand sign change meaning because of face/head/mouth movement?

5. Face & Mouth

17. How important are facial expressions?
18. How important are head movements/position?
19. How important are mouth/lip movements?
20. Are mouth movements necessary for any signs?
21. Are face/head/mouth movements used for grammar, emotion, emphasis, or meaning?

6. Emotion

22. Does emotion change meaning, or mainly show feeling/tone?
23. Which emotions are important to recognise?
24. Can ignoring facial/emotional expression cause misunderstanding?

7. Human Translation

25. Does an interpreter translate sign-by-sign or meaning/context?
26. How are differences in sign order vs Bengali/English order handled?
27. How are face, head, mouth and body movements included?
28. How are unknown/regional signs handled?

8. Bidirectional Communication

29. At a doctor/hospital, how does Deaf ↔ hearing communication normally happen?
30. In court/police/legal situations, how is Deaf ↔ hearing communication handled?
31. Is a human interpreter normally required?
32. How does the interpreter handle Sign → Bengali/English?
33. How does the interpreter handle Bengali/English → Sign?
34. Is translation word-by-word or meaning-based?
35. How is a hearing person's question converted into sign?
36. How is a Deaf person's answer converted into Bengali/English?
37. Are special requirements needed for medical/legal/emergency communication?
38. What mistakes would be dangerous for an automatic translator?

9. Alphabet & Fingerspelling

39. Is fingerspelling used?
40. Which alphabet is used: Bengali, English, or another?
41. What is done for names/unknown words/no existing sign?

10. Books & Software

42. Is there a WBSL/West Bengal sign-language book or dictionary?
43. Which books/videos/websites are actually used?
44. Are there existing apps/software/digital dictionaries?
45. Are West Bengal regional signs documented anywhere?

11. Regional Variation

46. Do signs vary by district, school, age or community?
47. Can different people perform the same sign differently but mean the same thing?
48. How should multiple signs with the same meaning be handled?
49. How should an unknown/local sign be handled?

12. Computer Translator

50. What body information must a computer capture?
51. Which is most important: hand/fingers, movement, palm orientation, arm/shoulder, face, head, mouth, body, timing, context?
52. Which information can be ignored?
53. Which signs require video instead of a single image?
54. What are common mistakes made by human learners/computer systems?
55. If the system does not understand a sign, should it ask/mark unknown instead of guessing?
56. What important thing about real sign communication have we missed asking?
